# Uzbekistan driving licence research

_Last verified: 2026-08-31_

This document describes how a person currently obtains a driving licence in Uzbekistan, with emphasis on category B, the theoretical and practical examinations, and a proposed preparation product for `prava-bot`.

## 1. What changed in 2026

The process changed materially in 2026.

From 1 February 2026, applicants for categories A and B no longer have to complete the theoretical part of driver training at an auto school. They may study theory independently, online, or through an educational organisation.

Practical driving instruction is still mandatory for categories A and B and must be completed through a licensed educational organisation.

A new A1 subcategory also took effect from 1 January 2026. A1 candidates may prepare independently and do not have to complete the standard mandatory driver-training programme before taking the theory and practical exams.

Official sources:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/ru/document/138
- https://gov.uz/oz/xorazm/news/view/97874
- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/news/2520

## 2. Normal category B flow

For a first-time category B applicant, the current flow is:

1. Meet the category B eligibility requirements.
2. Pass the required medical examination and obtain a valid 083/h medical certificate.
3. Learn road theory. This may now be done independently for category B.
4. Complete the mandatory practical driving course at a licensed driving school or training organisation.
5. Apply for admission to the exams.
6. Pass the theoretical exam.
7. Pass the practical exam.
8. Apply for issuance of the national driving licence.

The national driving licence is issued for 10 years.

Official sources:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/144
- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/3388
- https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/255

## 3. Minimum age and common categories

Government guidance currently lists these minimum ages:

| Category | Minimum age | Typical vehicle |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A | 16 | motorcycles |
| B | 18 | passenger vehicles up to 3,500 kg and up to 8 passenger seats, excluding the driver |
| C | 18 | vehicles above 3,500 kg |
| D | 21 | passenger vehicles with more than 8 passenger seats |

Source:

- https://gov.uz/ru/advice/73/document/138
- https://gov.uz/ru/advice/73/document/137

## 4. Medical certificate 083/h

Applicants must be medically fit to drive and need a valid medical certificate commonly referred to as form 083/h or form 083.

The medical conclusion is entered into the electronic system. The certificate can then be obtained electronically through my.gov.uz where available.

Sources:

- https://gov.uz/ru/advice/NaN/document/142
- https://gov.uz/ru/ssv/news/view/35816
- https://my.gov.uz/ru/news/f587408b-86be-4f9c-8633-b9221f40494e

## 5. Theoretical exam format

The current official format is:

- 20 electronic test questions;
- questions may be animated;
- each question has between 2 and 5 answer choices;
- exactly one answer is correct;
- total exam time is 25 minutes;
- the exam ends automatically when the time expires;
- the result is scored automatically;
- after completion, the system displays the result, correct/incorrect answers and time spent.

For the normal categories, including category B, the candidate must answer at least 18 of 20 questions correctly.

That means a category B candidate may make at most 2 mistakes.

For A1, the passing threshold is lower: at least 16 of 20 correct.

Official source:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/3383

Additional source:

- https://asdr.gov.uz/ru/sozdany-lgoty-po-upravleniyu-transportnymi-sredstvami/

### Time per question

The system provides one 25-minute timer for the complete 20-question exam rather than a separate timer for every question.

Average available time if divided equally is:

`25 minutes / 20 questions = 75 seconds per question`.

A preparation product should therefore train users to answer most familiar questions well below 75 seconds so that they have time to inspect harder traffic situations carefully.

## 6. What kinds of questions appear

Official public guidance defines the exam format but does not publish a guaranteed distribution such as "4 road-sign questions, 3 intersection questions, 2 first-aid questions" for every exam.

Therefore the product should not claim that the real exam contains a fixed number of questions from each topic unless an official current source publishes such a blueprint.

For preparation, however, the question bank should be organised into learning sections based on the Road Traffic Rules and the concepts needed to solve the exam.

Recommended preparation sections:

## 7. Exam behaviour and restrictions

During the official theory exam, the candidate may not use:

- study materials;
- a phone;
- information-retrieval devices;
- communication with another person;
- other technical means that could provide assistance.

The test is intended to assess independent knowledge.

Source:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/3383

## 8. Exam sequence

The order is mandatory:

1. theory;
2. practical driving.

A candidate who fails theory is not admitted to the practical exam.

The positive theory result remains valid for 2 months. If the candidate does not pass the practical exam within that period, theory must be taken again.

Source:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/144

## 9. Retakes

Current Ministry of Justice guidance states:

- a failed theoretical exam may be retaken from the next working day;
- a failed practical exam may be retaken after at least 7 calendar days;
- the theory result remains valid for 2 months while the candidate is trying to pass practice.

Source:

- https://advice.adliya.uz/oz/document/144

## 10. Practical exam

The practical exam takes place after theory is passed.

Government descriptions of automated exam centres show a practical exam on a dedicated course/autodrome where the candidate drives the vehicle alone and the system records mistakes automatically.

One government description of the Bukhara examination centre states that:

- the candidate receives instructions before the practical exam;
- a video explains which exercises must be performed and in which sequence;
- the candidate drives alone;
- mistakes generate penalty points;
- the exam is passed if penalty points do not exceed 99 points.

Source:

- https://gov.uz/oz/buxoro/news/view/30603

### Product warning

The exact practical-course layout, exercise set and scoring implementation should not be hard-coded globally from one examination centre article.

Before building a practical-exam simulator, we should separately verify the current official practical examination specification and whether all licensed centres use the same course, sequence and penalty matrix.

Until that research is complete, the bot should present the 99-point information as an example documented for an automated exam centre, not as an unqualified nationwide promise.

## 11. Applying for the exams

The Unified Portal has an online service for applying for admission to the driving exams:

- https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/482

At the time of this research, the portal displayed:

- online application completion time: about 10 minutes;
- service processing period: up to 3 days;
- portal charge: 0.09 BHM;
- displayed amount: 37,080 UZS;
- medical form 083 among the required documents.

The page still contains wording about a driver-training completion certificate that may not fully reflect the new 2026 theory self-study rules for A and B. Product answers should prefer the newer rules and explain this discrepancy if a user encounters it.

Monetary values should be treated as dynamic because BHM and service tariffs can change.

## 12. Receiving the licence

After passing the required exams, the applicant can use the government licence-issuance service:

- https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/255

The portal describes this process:

1. authenticate and submit an application;
2. use the ID/passport photo or attend the appropriate service point for a new photo;
3. provide the required signature;
4. pay the issuance fee;
5. collect the licence or request postal delivery where offered.

The licence is valid for 10 years.

## 13. Proposed product: preparation mini-site + Telegram bot

The strongest product is not a pure chatbot and not a pure static test website.

I would build one shared preparation platform with two interfaces:

- a mobile-first mini website for actual studying and exams;
- a Telegram bot for reminders, quick quizzes, progress and re-entry into the website.

The website is better for image-heavy road situations, timed exams and statistics. Telegram is better for retention and daily use.

## 14. Core user journey

### First launch

Ask only what is necessary:

1. language: Uzbek, Russian, possibly English;
2. category: default B;
3. target exam date, optional;
4. whether the user is starting from zero or already preparing.

Then run a short diagnostic test, for example 10 questions.

Do not force a long registration form before the first question.

### Home screen

The user should immediately see:

- readiness percentage;
- recent mock-exam score;
- weak topics;
- daily goal;
- one large "Continue preparation" button;
- one large "Start exam simulation" button.

Example:

```text
Ready for exam: 72%

Weak topics:
- Intersections 58%
- Parking 61%

Today: 12 / 20 questions

[ Continue ]
[ Mock exam: 20 questions / 25 min ]
```

## 15. Study modes

### Mode A: Learn by topic

User picks a section such as "Priority and intersections".

Flow:

1. very short rule explanation;
2. one visual example;
3. question;
4. answer;
5. explanation of why the selected answer is right or wrong;
6. next question.

Avoid making users read a long textbook before practicing.

### Mode B: Mistakes

Every incorrectly answered question goes into a mistake queue.

The system should distinguish:

- one accidental mistake;
- repeated misunderstanding;
- slow answer despite being correct.

Repeated mistakes should return through spaced repetition.

### Mode C: Road signs trainer

A fast swipe/card interface:

- show sign;
- user chooses meaning;
- instant correction;
- repeat difficult signs more often.

### Mode D: Situation trainer

For intersections and manoeuvres, display a road diagram or short animation and ask:

- who goes first;
- whether the manoeuvre is permitted;
- where the car may stop;
- which trajectory is legal.

### Mode E: Real exam simulation

This mode should reproduce the verified official theory format as closely as legally and technically possible:

- 20 questions;
- 25-minute global timer;
- 2–5 answers;
- one correct answer;
- normal category B pass threshold: 18/20;
- no hints during the simulation;
- final result only after submission or time expiry.

Result screen:

```text
18 / 20 — PASS
Time: 17:42

Mistakes:
- Q7 Intersections
- Q14 Stopping and parking

Average answer time: 53 sec
Exam readiness: 81%
```

Then offer:

`[ Review mistakes ]`

## 16. Readiness score

A raw percentage of correct answers is not enough.

The readiness model should include:

- recent mock-exam results;
- performance per topic;
- repeated mistakes;
- answer speed;
- number of questions seen;
- how recently the user studied.

Suggested logic for MVP:

```text
40% mock exam performance
30% topic mastery
20% mistake recovery
10% consistency/recency
```

A user should not get a 95% readiness score after answering only 20 easy questions.

Suggested gate before showing "exam ready":

- at least 3 recent mock exams;
- at least 2 of the last 3 at 18/20 or higher;
- no major topic below 70%;
- enough unique questions attempted to avoid memorising a tiny sample.

## 17. Explanations are the main differentiator

Many test products become question dumps. `prava-bot` should focus on teaching the rule behind the answer.

After a wrong answer, show:

1. correct answer;
2. one-sentence reason;
3. relevant rule;
4. optional expanded explanation;
5. optionally a second generated example using the same rule.

Example structure:

```text
Your answer: Car A
Correct: Car B

Why:
Car B is travelling on the priority road. The direction of the priority road is shown by the supplementary plate.

Remember:
At this intersection, vehicle priority is determined by the priority-road signs before the general right-hand rule is applied.
```

LLM-generated explanations must be grounded in the stored rule/source rather than generated from memory alone.

## 18. Question-bank data model

Each question should be structured rather than stored as a block of text.

Example:

```json
{
  "id": "uz-b-int-000123",
  "category": "B",
  "topic": "intersections",
  "subtopic": "priority-road",
  "language": "uz",
  "question": "...",
  "media": {
    "type": "image",
    "asset": "..."
  },
  "options": [
    {"id": "a", "text": "..."},
    {"id": "b", "text": "..."},
    {"id": "c", "text": "..."}
  ],
  "correct_option": "b",
  "explanation": "...",
  "rule_refs": ["YHQ:section-x:clause-y"],
  "source_refs": ["..."],
  "difficulty": 2,
  "version": 1,
  "verified_at": "2026-08-31"
}
```

Keep content and translations versioned so that rule changes can invalidate or update affected questions.

## 19. Question source strategy

Before importing any existing exam-question database, verify copyright/licensing and whether the database is officially released for reuse.

Safer architecture:

1. store the official Road Traffic Rules and regulations as source material;
2. create original practice questions that test the same rules;
3. use official diagrams/signs where reuse is legally permitted;
4. mark every practice question with the rule it teaches;
5. separately integrate an official question bank only if its reuse rights and technical source are confirmed.

The app should never claim that an internally generated question is an "official exam question" unless it actually comes from a verified official bank.

## 20. Telegram bot role

Telegram should be a companion, not the entire product.

Useful commands/actions:

```text
/start
Today’s 5 questions
My mistakes
Mock exam
My progress
Weak topics
Exam checklist
```

Daily message example:

```text
You have 4 weak intersection questions due today.
Last mock: 17/20.

[ Practice 5 min ]
```

A Telegram Web App button can open the mini-site directly inside Telegram so the user does not feel they are moving to a separate product.

## 21. Exam-day checklist feature

The product should also answer process questions, not only theory questions.

A separate checklist can show:

- medical certificate status;
- practical-course completion;
- exam application status;
- identity document reminder;
- theory result validity window after passing;
- next practical retake date if practice was failed;
- final licence issuance step.

This turns the product from a test app into a complete "get my licence" assistant.

## 22. MVP recommendation

Do not start by building every category or every feature.

Recommended MVP:

### Scope

- category B only;
- Uzbek and Russian;
- mobile-first web app;
- Telegram login/bot integration;
- topic practice;
- mistakes/review;
- mock exam with official 20-question / 25-minute format;
- progress dashboard;
- exam-process guide.

### First content target

A useful initial target would be roughly:

- 12–15 major topic groups;
- 30–50 verified original practice questions per major topic where appropriate;
- enough total variety that repeated mock exams do not immediately become memorisation exercises.

Do not optimise for a large raw question count before explanations and rule references are reliable.

## 23. Suggested technical architecture

One possible lightweight stack:

```text
Frontend / Telegram Web App
  Next.js
       |
       v
Backend API
  FastAPI or Next.js API
       |
       +--> PostgreSQL
       |      users
       |      questions
       |      attempts
       |      topic_mastery
       |      mock_exams
       |
       +--> question/rule content
       |
       +--> Telegram Bot
```

Important design rule: exam scoring and question correctness must be deterministic application logic. Do not ask an LLM whether the user's selected option is correct.

Use an LLM only for tasks such as:

- explaining a verified answer in simpler language;
- translating explanations;
- answering follow-up questions using retrieved rules;
- generating draft practice questions that are then validated before publication.

## 24. Product metrics

Useful metrics:

- diagnostic-to-first-practice conversion;
- questions answered per active day;
- 7-day return rate;
- percentage of users completing a mock exam;
- average mock score improvement;
- mistake recovery rate;
- number of users reaching the readiness threshold;
- reported real exam pass rate, if users voluntarily provide it.

The most meaningful outcome metric is eventually:

`percentage of prepared users who report passing the official theory exam on the first attempt`.

## 25. Open research tasks before production

The next research should verify:

1. the current official Road Traffic Rules text and stable clause identifiers for citation inside explanations;
2. whether an official reusable digital question bank exists;
3. whether official questions/images/animations can legally be reproduced;
4. the exact current practical-exam exercise list for category B;
5. the nationwide practical penalty-point matrix;
6. current exam-centre prices by region;
7. available examination centres and whether they expose schedules/booking APIs;
8. languages available in the official exam interface;
9. whether question selection follows any officially defined topic distribution;
10. exact rules governing practical training hours for category B after the 2026 theory change.
